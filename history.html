✍️ Term Origin

 
  • Aurora Borealis meaning Northern Dawn.
  • Aurora named after the Roman goddess of the dawn and Boreas, Greek name for the north wind.
 

📖 Discovery

 
  • Ancient humans likely observed auroras for thousands of years.
  • Galileo Galilei coined the term Aurora Borealis in 1619.
  • Early explanations ranged from divine signs to supernatural omens.
  • The scientific explanation wasn’t fully developed until the 20th century.
 

🧙‍♂️ Myths, Legends, and Cultural Beliefs

 

Cultures across the world created stories around the Northern Lights:

 
  • Norse: Lights reflected Valkyries leading fallen warriors to Valhalla.
  • Finnish: Caused by the mythical "Firefox" sweeping snowflakes into the sky with its tail.
  • Inuit: Spirits playing games or communicating with the living.
  • Japanese: Conceiving a child under the lights brings good fortune.
