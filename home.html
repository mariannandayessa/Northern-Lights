🌌 What Are the Northern Lights?

 

The Northern Lights, or Aurora Borealis, are one of nature’s most breathtaking spectacles—a radiant dance of color that paints the night sky in shifting waves of green, pink, purple, and blue. These glowing curtains of light ripple across the horizon, casting a surreal glow over snow-covered landscapes and quiet, icy lakes. Often seen near the Arctic Circle, the aurora has fascinated humans for thousands of years, inspiring awe, wonder, and legend.
